Crosswind: Airport Tycoon · Rushing Technologies

Need help? Report a problem or ask a question on our support board. Include your device model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Restoring purchases

Open EMPIRE in the game, scroll to the Shop and tap Restore purchases. One-time purchases (the Founder's Pack and Golden Runway) come back on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. Gems travel with your iCloud save.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your saved game

Your airports are saved on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud so they follow you to a new iPhone or iPad. Settings > Erase everything deletes both.

Your account

Signing in with Apple is optional and only needed for friends. Friends > Delete account erases your account, your friend code and every friendship from our server at once.
