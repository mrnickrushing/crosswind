Privacy Policy

Crosswind: Airport Tycoon · Rushing Technologies · Last updated November 10, 2026

Short version: Crosswind doesn't need an account (one is optional, for friends), doesn't show ads, doesn't track you across apps and doesn't sell any data. It sends anonymous play statistics, which you can turn off in Settings.

Your saved game

Your progress (your airports, team, research and settings) is saved on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud storage so it can continue on your other devices. iCloud is Apple's service; we don't receive or see your save.

Account and friends (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the game an anonymous identifier for your Apple ID and, if you share it, your first name (used only as your manager's name, which you can change). We never receive your email address. To let you add friends, the game sends our own server (run on Cloudflare) a one-way hash of that identifier, the manager's name you chose, and your airport's name, star rating and flight count. The server gives you a six-letter friend code. Friends you add can see your manager's name and those airport figures, and when you last played; nothing else. There is no chat. We do not store your IP address.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game records on your device which purchases it has delivered so nothing is granted twice.

Team crates

Crates are opened with gems, which are earned by playing and can also be bought. The chance of each rarity is shown in the game before you open one.

Anonymous play statistics

To see where players get stuck, the game sends small anonymous events to our own server (run on Cloudflare): for example that a goal was reached, a star gained or an airport sold. Each event also says the game version, the device model and system version, the language setting, and a frame-rate sample. Events carry a random identifier made on your device, which is hashed on your device before it is sent. They aren't linked to your name, Apple ID, purchases or advertising identifier, we don't store your IP address, they aren't shared with anyone or used to track you, and they're deleted after a year. You can turn this off at any time in Settings ("Share anonymous play statistics").

Reminders

If you allow notifications, reminders (the builders are done, a free crate is ready) are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings.

What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier, we don't show ads, and we don't share or sell data.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Friends > Delete account erases your account, your friend code and every friendship from our server at once. Settings > Erase everything deletes your saved game on the device and in iCloud and gives the game a new anonymous statistics identifier. You can also remove the app's access to your Apple ID in the iPhone's Settings (Apple ID > Sign-In & Security > Sign in with Apple).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: Crosswind support.
